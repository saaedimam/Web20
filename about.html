About Kattali Textile Ltd.

Excellence in textile manufacturing since 1998

Our Story

From humble beginnings to industry leadership

Founded in 1998, Kattali Textile Ltd. has grown from a small local textile manufacturer to one of Bangladesh's leading B2B textile companies. Our journey began with a simple vision: to create high-quality textiles while maintaining sustainable and ethical manufacturing practices.

Today, we serve over 500 clients across 35 countries, producing more than 50 million yards of premium textiles annually. Our commitment to innovation, sustainability, and excellence has made us a trusted partner for international brands and a publicly traded company on both the Chittagong and Dhaka Stock Exchanges.

🎯

Our Mission

To provide sustainable, high-quality textile solutions that meet international standards while contributing to Bangladesh's economic growth and environmental preservation.

👁️

Our Vision

To be the leading sustainable textile manufacturer in South Asia, setting industry standards for quality, innovation, and environmental responsibility.

⚖️

Our Values

Integrity, sustainability, innovation, quality, and social responsibility guide every decision we make and every product we create.
